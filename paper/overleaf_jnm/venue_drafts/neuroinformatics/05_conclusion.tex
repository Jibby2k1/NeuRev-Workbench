\section{Conclusion}

NeuRev Workbench makes observation, geometry, biological identity, candidate
state, and evidence status explicit and separately auditable. In the case study,
that design prevented unmatched candidates from becoming false positives,
prevented identity-crossing recentering from becoming recovery, and kept
computational simulation distinct from biological validation. Source-bound
facts, venue-only figure manifests, and fail-closed packaging reduce narrative
drift, but they do not replace author review. Independent adjudication,
bounded-field truth, cross-recording transfer, portable example data, and a clean
public release remain the decisive gates for a general neuroinformatics claim.
