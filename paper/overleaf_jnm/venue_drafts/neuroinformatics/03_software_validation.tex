human truth, independent-recording transfer, and release portability remain
open.

The package therefore demonstrates a usable provenance architecture and catches
the concrete errors found during manuscript audit: stale cohort counts,
occurrence-versus-site denominator drift, per-lane-versus-global budget
conflation, provisional operator promotion, and missing visuals. It does not yet
demonstrate third-party installation, interoperability across acquisition
formats, multi-recording scale, or independent regeneration by another group.
