\section{Conclusion}

Sparse-positive calcium imaging can support rigorous discovery and method
evaluation when unknown candidates, immutable measurements, and provisional
identities remain distinct. In this recording, strong known-positive recovery
coexisted with provisional likely-neuron calls in a selected unmatched batch,
identity-crossing recentering gains, and identity-clear proposal absences.
Exact-truth simulation supported complementary feature roles while exposing
close-source identity and stopping limits. Identity-safe provenance therefore
changes what can be claimed now: independent adjudication, bounded truth, and
cross-recording transfer remain the next decisive evidence.
