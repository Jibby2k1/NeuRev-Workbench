\abstract{
Incomplete neuronal annotations create inferential and provenance problems for
calcium-imaging software: unmatched detections are not verified negatives,
coordinate updates can silently change biological identity, and results can be
detached from the data and configuration that produced them. NeuRev Workbench
implements immutable observation identifiers, separately hashed geometry and
trace records, explicit canonical-identity relations, positive--unlabeled
estimands, versioned artifacts, and human-gated evidence status. We demonstrate
the system on one zebrafish fluorescence recording with
\VenueCohortBursts{} burst intervals, \VenueCohortOccurrences{} confirmed
neuron--burst occurrences, and \VenueCohortSites{} immutable sites. At a
per-lane \(B=\VenuePerLaneBudget\) operating point, the any-lane union recovered
\VenueStrictRecovered/\VenueCohortOccurrences{} occurrences. Among the
\VenueStrictMissed{} all-lane misses, \VenueIdentityCollision{} apparent local
improvements crossed into labeled identities and \VenueIdentityClear{} remained
identity-clear proposal-absence hypotheses. In a separate selected batch,
one blinded reviewer called \VenueReviewDefinite{} of
\VenueReviewedCandidates{} unmatched sites definite and
\VenueReviewProbable{} probable; the fraction is not precision. Registered
exact-truth simulations supplied functional validation while keeping
computational and biological evidence tiers separate. A source-bound manuscript
build synchronizes repeated facts and figures and rejects stale cohorts,
placeholder figures, and ambiguous budget wording. Public release,
independent adjudication, bounded-field truth, and cross-recording transfer
remain explicit gates.
}
