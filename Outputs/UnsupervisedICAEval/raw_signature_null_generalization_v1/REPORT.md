# Raw signature null/generalization assay

Five-fold ROI-held-out evaluation assigned **S0_no_reproducible_signature**. Mean held-out event correlation was 0.8256. The event-minus-same-ROI-shift delta was 0.8249 (95% ROI-bootstrap CI 0.7267, 0.9045; p=0.0001). The event-minus-annulus delta was -0.0131 (95% CI -0.0277, 0.0008; p=0.9523). This is within-recording, label-anchored morphology evidence; it is not a precision or cross-recording claim.
